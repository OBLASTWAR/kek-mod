* New Remap Vote lobby option: at the start of turn 10 everyone votes on restarting the game on a new map. On by default, and it needs everyone to vote yes; the host can set it to Off, Majority or Majority +1/+2/+3.
* Ending your turn now shows you any trade offers you haven't looked at yet (RESPOND TO DEALS). It never stops you ending the turn.
* New Dim Enemy Unit Flags lobby option: other players' unit flags dim when their units are out of moves, like your own. Off by default.
* Fixed a crash when a unit was removed while its flag was updating, e.g. using a Great Scientist.
* Units you can't see no longer leave a gap in a stack of unit flags, and hovering a carrier's or transport's cargo count lists the units aboard.
* The main menu shows your KEK Mod version and tells you when a newer one is out.
* Kicking a player in the lobby no longer wipes the chat.
* The vote screens (IRR, CC, SCRAP, Remap and the CAST VOTE button) are translated into every game language.
